TECHNICAL MEMORANDUM
Map pictures in PuNoObservation: licences to settle before daily use

For:     CENRO officials and the office IT staff
From:    PuNoObservation project team
Date:    October 2026
Action:  Choose a licensed map source before the system goes into
         daily office use


IN SHORT
--------
The maps in PuNoObservation show a street map and an optional
satellite view. Both are loaded from outside services. The system
currently uses their free public servers. That is allowed for
development and demonstrations.

If a CENRO office uses the system every day, the office should switch
to a source it is licensed to use. The switch is a settings change
only. No programming is needed.


WHAT THE MAPS USE TODAY
-----------------------
Every map in the system draws its background from small picture
squares called "tiles". The boundaries, sites and pins on top are the
system's own data and are not affected by this memo.

Street map: OpenStreetMap public tile servers
  - The map data is free to use, with credit to OpenStreetMap.
  - The servers are run by volunteers. Their Tile Usage Policy asks
    heavy users to use another provider or host their own tiles.
  - They may slow down or block heavy users without notice.

Satellite view: Esri World Imagery
  - Helps staff recognise real land, roads and rivers when marking
    sites.
  - Esri expects apps in production to use an ArcGIS account and key.
  - ArcGIS Location Platform has a free monthly allowance of map tiles.


OPTIONS FOR THE OFFICE
----------------------
Pick one option for the satellite view and one for the street map.
They can be decided separately.

1. Esri key (suggested)
   What to do: Create an ArcGIS Location Platform account, make an API
               key, and give it to IT. The satellite view keeps
               working as it does now.
   Cost:       Free within the monthly allowance.

2. Government source
   What to do: Ask NAMRIA which Geoportal Philippines map services the
               office may use, and under what terms.
   Cost:       Depends on NAMRIA.

3. No satellite
   What to do: IT turns the satellite view off. Only the street map
               shows.
   Cost:       None.

4. Street map provider
   What to do: Use a commercial tile provider that serves OpenStreetMap
               maps, or host the tiles on an office server.
   Cost:       Many providers have a free tier.


WHAT IT STAFF CHANGE
--------------------
The settings live in the .env file on the server. A full example with
notes is in .env.example in the project. After editing, restart the
application.

  # Satellite, with the office's ArcGIS key
  MAP_SATELLITE_TILES=https://server.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer/tile/{z}/{y}/{x}?token=YOUR_ARCGIS_KEY
  MAP_SATELLITE_ATTRIBUTION=Imagery &copy; Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics

  # Or, to turn the satellite view off
  # MAP_SATELLITE_TILES=

  # Street map from the office's chosen provider
  MAP_STREET_TILES=https://tiles.example-provider.com/{z}/{x}/{y}.png?key=YOUR_KEY
  MAP_STREET_ATTRIBUTION=&copy; OpenStreetMap contributors

{z} {x} {y} are filled in by the map and must stay as written. The
credit line appears in the corner of every map, as the licences
require.


BEFORE GOING LIVE
-----------------
[ ] A satellite option is chosen: Esri key, NAMRIA source, or off.
[ ] A street map option is chosen: provider or office-hosted tiles.
[ ] The keys are stored only in .env on the server, never shared by
    email or chat.
[ ] IT has restarted the application and checked the GIS map, the
    request form and a request detail page.
[ ] The credit line in the corner of the map names the right source.


PLEASE CHECK THE CURRENT TERMS
------------------------------
Esri and OpenStreetMap update their terms and free allowances from
time to time. This memo describes them as the project team understood
them in October 2026. It is not legal advice. Confirm the details on
each provider's website before signing up.
