What is this page for?

Oversight. It lists the requests and monitoring reports CENRO offices have already approved, in one place.

Why can't I approve or edit anything here?

The page is read-only on purpose. Decisions stay with the CENRO that owns the record. Boundary publication has its own page.

Why don't I see every CENRO?

A CENRO account sees its own office only. A province-wide account sees every CENRO, including offices with no approved work yet.

Why is a report I know about missing?

Only approved reports and requests appear. Pending, rejected or cancelled ones do not.

How is "Mean survival" worked out?

The average survival rate of all approved reports. "Below {{ threshold }}%" counts the reports under the DENR threshold.

Why does the table say "Showing the most recent"?

Long tables are cut to the newest rows to keep the page quick. The numbers at the top still count everything.
