{# FAQ for Boundary Publications (admin.publications). Provincial administrators only. Behaviour described lives in admin.publish_boundary. #}
Where do these boundaries come from?

From monitoring reports. A field officer photographs the site's corners with GPS, and the system joins them into a boundary.

A boundary appears here once a CENRO approves that report.

Why is publishing separate from the CENRO's approval?

A published boundary is shown to every map user, across the province. So it gets its own provincial check.

What should I check before publishing?
  • Difference: how far the captured area is from the contracted area. Over 10% needs a closer look.
  • Corner photos flagged: photos with missing GPS or other problems.
  • The shape on the full report looks like the real site.
What does each button do?
  • Publish to map: the boundary becomes the site's official boundary and shows on the map. It replaces any boundary published before.
  • Decline: keeps the boundary off the map. A reason is required.
  • Withdraw from map: takes a published boundary off the map and puts it back in the queue.
Who is told about my decision?

The field officer who captured the boundary, and the CENRO administrator who approved the report.
