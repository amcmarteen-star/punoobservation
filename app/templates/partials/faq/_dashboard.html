Why is a location approximate?

Sites imported from DENR records have no coordinates. They are drawn as a hollow marker with "N sites" at the centre of their barangay.

A solid dot is a real position: a published GPS boundary, or the pin a requester dropped. A site gets one once the province publishes its boundary.

{% if is_admin %}
What is simulated data?

Made-up sites, loaded to show what a province-wide rollout would look like. They are labelled Simulated and drawn dashed on the map.

They are never used in the story, the chart titles, the ranking, the gauge or the scatter. Only real sites are.

Why do I only see some municipalities?

A CENRO account sees only its own CENRO's area. Provincial and System Administrators see all of Pangasinan.

What are the boxes above the numbers?

What is waiting: requests, monitoring reports and boundaries. Each box opens its queue.

Request and report review is a CENRO task, so for a Provincial Administrator those two open the Provincial Overview.

Reading the charts

Why does each chart title read like a sentence?

The title states what the chart shows, worked out from the data, for example "Contracting fell from 940 ha in 2017 to 120 ha in 2025". The current year is marked "(to date)" and left out of that trend.

Where does the survival rate come from?

From approved monitoring reports. When a CENRO approves a report, its survival rate is saved to the site. The target is the DENR {{ threshold }}%.

Why does a municipality say "Too few sites"?

A municipality is judged only when at least 3 of its sites have survival data. With fewer it is grey, never orange, and left out of the story and the scatter.

What is cost per hectare?

The three-year project cost divided by the contracted area. Big differences can come from terrain, species or how hard the site is to reach.

How is "Where to plant next" worked out?

Every barangay gets a suitability score from the species recommender. The list shows well-suited barangays that have no site yet.

How up to date are the numbers?

They are worked out fresh every time you open the page. Reload it to see the latest.
