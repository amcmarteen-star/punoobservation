{# FAQ for Create site from request (admin.create_site_from_request). Behaviour described lives in that route's docstring. #}
Why is it a temporary site?

The land has not been checked yet. The site gives the field officer something to visit and record against.

It stays out of the dashboard, the heat map, reports and the public map until the request is approved. It reaches the public map when the province publishes its boundary.

What gets created?
  • The site, in the request's barangay, under your CENRO.
  • A planting record holding the target seedlings. Field officers need it to submit monitoring reports.
  • An assignment for the officer you choose. They get a notification and an email.
Is the requester's drawn area used as the boundary?

No. It shows on the site as a proposed area only. It never appears on the GIS map.

The official boundary comes later, from a field officer's GPS corner photos, once the province publishes it.

What area should I enter?

The planned area. A GPS boundary measured later is stored separately and compared with this number.

Is the site code required?

No, but if you enter one it must not be used by another site.

The organization isn't in the list. What do I do?

The list shows only organizations in the request's barangay, as on the sign-up page.

Type the name under Add a new organization. It is used instead of the list.

Why must I choose a field officer?

Only an assigned officer can see the request and record a visit. You can assign more officers or later visits from the site's Site Visits page.

If no officer is listed, ask a system administrator to add one for your CENRO.
