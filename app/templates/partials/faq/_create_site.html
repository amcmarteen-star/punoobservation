{# FAQ for Create site from request (admin.create_site_from_request). Behaviour described lives in that route's docstring. #}
Why isn't the site created automatically when I approve?

A request does not carry everything a site needs, such as a name, an organisation and a planting date. The site also becomes an official record, so a person fills it in.

What gets created?
  • The site, in the request's barangay, under your CENRO.
  • A planting record holding the target seedlings. Field officers need it to submit monitoring reports.
Is the requester's drawn area used as the boundary?

No. It shows on the site as a proposed area only. It never appears on the GIS map.

The official boundary comes later, from a field officer's GPS corner photos, once the province publishes it.

What area should I enter?

The planned area. A GPS boundary measured later is stored separately and compared with this number.

Is the site code required?

No, but if you enter one it must not be used by another site.

The organisation isn't in the list. What do I do?

Type its name under Or add a new organisation. It is used instead of the list.
