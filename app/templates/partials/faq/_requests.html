Making a request

Which request type should I choose?
  • New Planting Site: you have land you want reforested. You must mark it on the map.
  • Adoption: you want to take care of a site that already exists.
  • Seedling Supply: you need seedlings.
  • Technical Assistance: you need advice or help from DENR staff.
How do I mark the site on the map?

Press Mark it on the map. Then either:

  • Drop a pin where the site is, or
  • Draw the plot by clicking each corner. This measures the area for you.

The pin must be inside the barangay you chose, and inside the plot if you drew one.

I don't know the size of the land. What do I put?

Type your best guess, or leave it blank. If you draw the plot on the map, the measured area is used instead.

How is "Estimated seedlings" worked out?

Area ÷ the space each seedling takes. Pick a planting type to use its standard spacing, or enter your own.

It is a planning estimate only, with no extra for seedlings that die and need replacing.

Which documents should I attach?

A photo or scan of your Indicative Management Plan (IMP), or other supporting documents.

Images or PDF only, up to {{ app_limits.maxRequestFiles }} files of {{ app_limits.maxFileMb }} MB each. It is optional, but a request with an IMP is reviewed faster.

After you submit

Who reviews my request?

The CENRO office for the barangay you chose. They are notified as soon as you submit.

What do the statuses mean?
  • Submitted: received, not opened yet.
  • Under Review: the CENRO is checking it.
  • Approved: accepted.
  • Rejected: not accepted. The reason is shown with it.

You get a notification every time the status changes.

Can I cancel a request?

Yes, while it is still Submitted or Under Review. Press Cancel on its row.

This deletes the request and its documents for good. Once it is approved or rejected, it can't be cancelled.

Can I edit a request after sending it?

No. Cancel it while it is still pending, then send a new one.
