{# FAQ for the Audit Log (admin.audit_log). System administrators only. #}
What is recorded here?

Important actions in the system: sign-ins and failed sign-ins, submitted reports and requests, review decisions, publications, imports and account changes.

Can entries be edited or deleted?

No. Each entry is written once and kept. Entries stay even when the user's account is deleted.

How do I find something specific?

Filter by action, user and period, or search. The tabs at the top jump to sign-ins and failed sign-ins.

Why can't I see older entries?

The page shows the 500 most recent matches. Narrow the period or the action to reach older ones.

What should I do about failed sign-ins?

A few are normal: people mistype passwords. Many for one account in a short time may mean someone is guessing. Check with the account holder.
