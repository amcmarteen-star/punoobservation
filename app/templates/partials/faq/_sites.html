{# FAQ for Reforestation Sites (dashboard.Reforestation_sites). Behaviour described lives in scope_sites, can_see_site and admin.export_sites_excel. #}
Which sites are listed?

Every reforestation site in your area. A CENRO account sees its own CENRO. Province-wide accounts see all of Pangasinan.

"Showing" at the top says which area you are looking at.

How do I find one site?

Use the Municipality filter, or search by name or code. Clear resets it.

What does "View" in the Map column do?

It opens the site on the GIS map. "No coordinates" means the site has no location recorded yet.

What does "From request" mean?

The site was created from a reforestation request, not imported from DENR records.

Its area is the requester's proposed area until the province publishes a GPS boundary.

How is the duration worked out?

From the contract start date to its end date, counted in whole months.

Why can't I see the contact person?

Contact details are private. Only staff (field officers and administrators) see them.

The sites you can see, in the DENR contract profile layout, with their reports and requests.
