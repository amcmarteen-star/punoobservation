Roles

What can each role do?
  • System Administrator: user accounts, the reference dataset and the audit log. Never reviews or publishes.
  • CENRO Administrator: reviews requests and monitoring reports for one CENRO.
  • Provincial Administrator: province-wide oversight, and publishes site boundaries.
  • Field Officer: submits monitoring reports.
  • Normal User: submits reforestation requests.
How do I make a Provincial Administrator?

Choose the Administrator role and set the jurisdiction to PENRO — Province-wide. Choosing a CENRO instead makes a CENRO Administrator.

Accounts

How does a new user get their password?

You set a temporary password when you add them. Give it to them directly, never by email.

They must choose their own password the first time they sign in.

Someone forgot their password. What do I do?

Press Reset password on their row and set a temporary one. It works once.

Should I deactivate or delete an account?

Deactivate in most cases. The person can no longer sign in, but their work stays linked to their name. You can reactivate them later.

Delete only for accounts with no reports, requests or published boundaries. It cannot be undone.

Why can't I deactivate my own account?

You would lock yourself out of this page. For the same reason, the last active system administrator can't be deactivated.
