{# FAQ for My Account (dashboard.my_account). Numbers come from app_limits (app/config.py). #}
How do I change my password?

Fill in the Change password form. You need your current password first.

A new password needs {{ app_limits.passwordMin }} to {{ app_limits.passwordMax }} characters, with at least one letter and one number.

Why must I change my password before I can do anything?

An administrator gave you a temporary password. The rest of the system stays locked until you choose your own.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Ask your system administrator to reset it. They give you a temporary password, which you change the next time you sign in.

Can I change my username, email or role?

Not here. Ask your system administrator.

What does Text size do?

It makes text and buttons bigger or smaller on every page.

It is saved on this device only. Set it again on another phone or computer.

Why was I signed out?

For safety, you are signed out after {{ app_limits.sessionIdleMinutes }} minutes without activity. Sign in again to continue.

What is Recent activity?

Things you did in the system, such as signing in, submitting reports and reviewing work.
