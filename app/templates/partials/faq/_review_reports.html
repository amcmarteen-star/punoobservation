Which reports do I see?

Monitoring reports for sites under your CENRO, submitted by field officers.

What does "flagged" mean in the Flags column?

How many photos have something to check. A photo is flagged when it:

  • has no GPS location or no date
  • was taken outside the site's barangay
  • was taken more than 60 days before the monitoring date, or after it
  • was already used, in this report or an earlier one

Flags are warnings, not proof. Open the report and decide.

What is Intensity?

How much of the site was counted. Plot surveys should reach {{ '%g'|format(sampling_rule.targetPct) }}%. A whole-site count is 100%.

What happens when I approve a report?

Its survival rate is saved to the site's planting record right away, and the dashboard uses it.

A captured boundary goes to the provincial administrator for publication.

Does the field officer know my decision?

Yes. They get a notification with your note. A rejection needs a note, so tell them what to fix.
