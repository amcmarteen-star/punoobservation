Joining

I don't have an invite link

Ask the leader of the organization you want to join. They copy it from My Organization.

If the organization has no leader yet, ask your CENRO office.

Until you join, you can still use the map and the species recommendations as an Individual.

The invite link says it no longer works

The leader made a new link, so the old one stopped working. Ask the leader for the new one.

My approval is taking long
  • Verify your email first. Leaders only see people whose email is verified. Look for the email we sent you.
  • Tell your leader you have asked to join. They approve you under My Organization.
  • If the organization has no leader, the CENRO approves you instead. Contact your CENRO office.

You get a notification once you are approved. Until then you are a Pending Member and cannot request sites.

Can I join two organizations?

No. Leave your current one first, from My Account. Then open the other organization's invite link.

For leaders

How do I invite members?

Copy the invite link on this page and send it to them. When they open it they sign up, or confirm if they already have an account.

They then wait for you to approve them. You are notified each time.

Making a new link stops the old one from working.

What do Forward, Return and Reject do?
  • Forward: sends the request to the CENRO. Its status becomes Pending CENRO.
  • Return: sends it back to the member for changes. Write what to change; the member sees your note.
  • Reject: ends the request. Write a reason; the member sees it.

The member is notified every time. A request you send yourself skips this step and goes straight to the CENRO.

How do I remove a member?

Use Remove next to their name. You cannot remove another leader; ask your CENRO.

How does someone become a leader?

Leaders are appointed by the CENRO on this page. An organization can have more than one leader.

To leave, the last leader must first ask the CENRO to appoint another.
