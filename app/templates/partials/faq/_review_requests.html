{# FAQ for Review Requests (admin.review_requests). CENRO administrators only. Behaviour described lives in admin.review_requests and admin.review_request. #}
Which requests do I see?

Requests from barangays in your CENRO's area. The list opens on Pending (Submitted and Under Review).

What does each status mean?
  • Under Review: you are still checking it. For a New Planting Site, you can now create a temporary site and assign an officer.
  • Approved: accepted.
  • Rejected: not accepted. A reason is required.
Does the requester know when I change the status?

Yes. They get a notification every time, with your note if you wrote one.

Where do I see the map, documents and contact details?

Press Details on the row. The full page shows the pin, the drawn area, attachments, and other sites and requests in the same barangay.

Can I change a decision?

Yes, by setting a new status. Once a site's boundary is published, its request must stay Approved. A site closed as Not Suitable can't change.

What if a requester cancels?

The request disappears from the list and you get a notification. Requesters can only cancel before a decision is made, and before a site is created from it.
