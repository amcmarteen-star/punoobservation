How do I mark notifications as read?

Opening one marks it read. The eye button on each row switches it between read and unread.

To do many at once, tick them and press Mark read, or press Mark all as read.

Can I delete notifications?

Yes. Use the bin button on a row, or tick several and press Delete. This cannot be undone.

How do I find an old notification?

Filter by type (Reports or Requests) and by time (Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days).

What does the bell in the top bar do?

It shows your newest notifications on any page. The red number is how many are unread.
