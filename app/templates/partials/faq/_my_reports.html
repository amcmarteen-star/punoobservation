{# FAQ for My Reports (dashboard.my_reports_page). Field officers only. #}
What is on this page?

Every monitoring report you have submitted, newest first, with its review status.

What do the statuses mean?
  • Pending: waiting for your CENRO to review it.
  • Approved: accepted. The site's survival rate now uses your figure.
  • Rejected: not accepted. The reviewer's reason is shown under the status.
My report was rejected. Can I edit it?

No. A submitted report cannot be changed.

Read the reason, fix the problem, then submit a new monitoring report.

What is Intensity?

How much of the site your counts covered. A whole-site count is 100%. Plot surveys aim for {{ '%g'|format(sampling_rule.targetPct) }}%.

How do I see a report's photos and plot counts?

Press View on its row.

Can I delete a report?

No. Reports are official records and stay in the system.
