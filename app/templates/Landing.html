About the Platform

One shared source of truth for reforestation data

PuNoObservation is a centralized platform built to help DENR, CENRO, environmental organizations, and community leaders monitor reforestation progress across Eastern Pangasinan. It replaces scattered, disconnected records with one shared system, making site data easier to access and decisions easier to make.

Who It's For

Built for your role

Every account is scoped to what that role actually needs to do.

Administrator

Full control over system operations -- manages site locations and user accounts, reviews reforestation site requests, and configures the reference dataset that powers the recommendation engine.

Field Officer

Focused on field-related work -- exploring the GIS map, checking registered reforestation sites, and pulling species recommendations for the barangays they're assigned to.

Normal User

For community members and partner organizations -- browses the GIS map and registered sites, then submits reforestation site requests for an administrator to review.
