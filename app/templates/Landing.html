Get to know us

One platform for smarter reforestation

PuNoObservation is a web-based decision support platform that helps DENR, CENRO, environmental organizations, and community leaders manage reforestation projects across Eastern Pangasinan.

It centralizes planting site records, geospatial mapping, tree inventories, species recommendations, and progress monitoring into one shared system, making environmental data more accessible and decision-making more efficient.

A Web-Based Decision Support Platform for Tree Species Recommendation and Reforestation Management with Geospatial Visualization in Eastern Pangasinan.

Elevation is a model input, so a lowland site and an upland site never rank the same.

Integrated features for reforestation management

Available features depend on your account role. Administrators, Field Officers, and Normal Users each see a tailored set of tools.
